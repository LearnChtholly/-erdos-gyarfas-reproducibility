
Erdos-Gyarfas suppression theorem candidate T10
================================================

Setting
-------
Let G be a counterexample to the Erdos-Gyarfas conjecture, chosen first with
minimum |V(G)| and then with minimum |E(G)|. Let

    A = V_3(G),   B = V_{>=4}(G),
    a = |A|,      b = |B|,
    E = sum_{v in B}(d_G(v)-4),
    z = #{u in A : d_{G[A]}(u)=3}.

Accepted structural input from Carr:
(1) B is independent.
(2) Every vertex has a degree-3 neighbor.
(3) Every proper subgraph has minimum degree at most 2.

Suppression graph
-----------------
For every u in A with d_{G[A]}(u)=1, suppress the B-u-B path to one edge.
The resulting graph Q on B is simple and 2-degenerate. Moreover, any
2^k-cycle in Q lifts to a 2^{k+1}-cycle in G, so Q is relevant
power-of-two-cycle-free.

Exact identity
--------------
Let x,y,z denote the numbers of A-vertices with d_{G[A]} equal to 1,2,3.
Since B is independent,

    2x+y = 4b+E,

and

    a=x+y+z.

Since e(Q)=x,

    a = 4b+E+z-e(Q).

Also x+z is even by the handshake lemma in G[A], hence

    e(Q) ≡ z (mod 2).

Finite extremal input
---------------------
Certified computation gives

    e(Q) <= 2b-8   for b>=14,
    e(Q) <= 2b-9   for b>=16.

Therefore

    a >= 2b+E+8+2 ceil(z/2)       (b>=14),

and

    a >= 2b+E+10+2 floor(z/2)      (b>=16).

Dangerous sectors for T10
-------------------------
To violate a>=2b+10 when b>=14, one must have b in {14,15}, z=0,
and E in {0,1}. In these sectors G[A] is a union of paths and cycles.

The four sectors
----------------
(14,0), (14,1), (15,0), (15,1)

have been reduced to finite suppression-lift searches over the certified
(14,20) and (15,22) suppression cores. Component-trigger constraints from
minimality and exact C4/C8/C16 local cycle oracles eliminate all cases.

Candidate conclusion
--------------------
If G is a vertex-minimal, then edge-minimal Erdos-Gyarfas counterexample and
b=|V_{>=4}(G)|>=14, then

    a=|V_3(G)| >= 2b+10.

Equivalently,

    |V_3(G)| >= ceil((2|V(G)|+10)/3)

whenever |V_{>=4}(G)|>=14.

Proof status
------------
The algebraic/suppression part is human-readable. The fixed-defect bounds and
danger-sector eliminations are computer-assisted and should be checked from
the attached independent verifiers before publication claims.
